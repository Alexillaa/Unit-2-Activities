POO's Activities & Examples throughout the Second Unit 
Activity 1: Display the model, color and Brand of a car. 
Activity 2: Greet a person and specify their name, years old and the city they are from. 
Activity 3: Convert Dollars to Pesos. 
Activity 4: Show the name of an employee and the salary, and the name, salary and department of a Manager. 
Activity 5: Calculate employees payment in which we have hourly paid and full time employee. 
Activity 6: Show the legend 'Generating Financial Report' and 'Repairing Machine'. 
Activity 7: Show the legend 'Engine Started'. 
Activity 8:Display the Title and Pages of a Book.
